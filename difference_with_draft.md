## Difference with draft

Following Joel's feedback, I revisited the draft and recognised that the problem definition was not sufficiently clear and that the experimental scope was too broad. After reconsidering the project, I changed the experimental objective and used a revised workflow to produce the final report.

The original Experiment 2 has been completely removed. The work previously described as Experiment 1 has been expanded with clearer explanations of the methods, the literature behind the processing steps, and the reasons for the experimental settings. The revised analysis follows the effect of changing heartbeat positions through respiratory clues, cycle counting and final respiratory-rate estimates, with particular attention to the complete a02 recording.

The final version therefore differs substantially from the draft in its research objective, experimental scope, method explanation and report structure.
